## Appendix C. What is still unsupported or uncertain

**Concepts with no research finding and no documented topic** (they rest on convention, on Tagg's taxonomy grouping, or on inference, and are marked so in their entries):
- **Luck and fortune**: nothing addresses luck. Only analogues: the comic mode's swerves (HAT23) and the cadence that lands in a far key (T156). Surprise itself was too unreliable to keep in a film rating study (EV).
- **Greed**: only Tagg's filing of the miser with weakness and pettiness (VVA 1268) and the gold-and-treasure prop field (VVA 2671), which has no tune data. The sounds (accumulating figures, glints) remain inference.
- **Fragility**, **Weariness**, **Bitterness and cynicism**: mood tags (AMM, MRX, YM, EV's sleepy pole) and Tagg's field placement, but no feature data.
- **Fire**: the mood side is now backed ("fiery" loads on GEMS power; MRX C5), and flicker is a documented fine-motor anaphone, but the specific sounds of flame (crackle, roar) are still inference; the game lava topic is a flagged stereotype.
- **Sickness**, **Captivity**, **Suspicion and jealousy**, **Guilt**: supported by Tagg's musogenic grouping (sickness and imprisonment with darkness and confinement, VVA 125; guilt and jealousy with madness, VVA 127) and some of his general statements, not by tune-by-tune evidence for the concept itself.
- **Undeath**: ombra gives a documented sound for ghosts and the solemn dead; "life's cues gone wrong" for walking corpses is still inference.

**Concepts resting on film or game convention only** (leitmotif catalogues or course notes, no reception data): Oath and vow, Artifacts and relics, Rescue, Arrival and homecoming, Desperation and the last stand, Family and kinship (partly Tagg's parental-love finding), Decline and fading (partly on LOTR notes tagged [A], the least reliable tier), The monster, The heavens and the planes (the MTTP topic is documented; Tagg's cosmic responses were weak).

**Conflicts and weak points in the sources**
- Cowen et al. tie scary music to harmonic dissonance (qualitative), while research §8.6 r12 finds scary music need not be dissonant. The Fear entry treats dissonance as optional.
- Hevner's cluster 1 mode weight is printed as "major 4" in the reproduction used and as minor elsewhere; it is small either way.
- "Entrancing" was the one of Cowen's 28 categories no music evoked reliably, so trance is hard to score on purpose.
- Schubert's 46-word grouping is unverified [R]; the GEMS-45 extra items and exact GEMS-25 wording were not available.
- Topic features marked [gen.] in `src-topics.md` (amoroso, romanza, pavane, polonaise rhythm, bardic and chivalric detail, Biedermeier, mazurka, tango, the Lebewohl voicing) are unchecked textbook knowledge.
- Unread Tagg chapters known only by title: "Eerie ice or fearful fog?", "Busy xylophones and comic bustle", "Pentatonicism and pastures", "Power chord threat", and MM ch. 12 on social anaphones in depth.
- Tagg's reception data come from 607 mostly Swedish listeners in the 1980s hearing Euro-American screen idioms; the Euro-funereal norm is explicitly not universal; the exotic topics are documented stereotypes with published critiques, kept as warnings (R12).

**Orchestra gaps** (no exact substitute; the stand-ins keep the carrying feature, R1): the theremin's continuous glide, sleigh bells, struck metal plates and chains, distorted guitar roughness, sul ponticello and string harmonics, the cimbalom's hammered tremble, the Hardanger fiddle's resonance, a boy soprano.

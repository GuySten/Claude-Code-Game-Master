# Play card

Ask these every scene and every round. Numbers: `design-directives.md`.

**Reading the round**
- Can each action work? If not: `gm-table.sh redo` with the reason, before any roll. (18)
- What does each player actually want from it? Ask if the goal is unclear. (10)
- Who hasn't had a beat lately? Who prefers the background? (38, 39, 49)

**Before a roll**
- Is anything at stake? No: it just happens. Yes: one roll. (65, 33)
- Ladder word from the task and the fiction, not from who rolls. Same as last time? (30)
- Have I said the danger and the kind of cost, and offered a way out? (10, 8)
- Open roll, or `--secret` only because the PCs couldn't know the result? (32, T1)

**Before a choice or a fight**
- Can they read it: size, gear, how others react, an honest tier if they look? (8, 15)
- Did a warning (sound, sight) come before the harm? (9)
- Is there a safe option, a risky one, and an exit? (11, 46)
- Zones, distances, cover named? Enemy labels and nerve shown this round? (73, 16)

**Clues**
- Did a PC look in the right place with a fitting skill? Then they find it, free. (20)
- Told to the whole table, finder named? Whisper only if the secret is the point. (22, T6)
- Which PC hasn't had a route yet? Is the party stuck? Bring a lead to them. (21, 26)

**After the roll / in the narration**
- Persisted before narrating? Money and items said with numbers? (74)
- New place's ways out recorded with `gm-location.sh connect`? (72)
- Everyone who contributed named? The success left intact, the cost elsewhere? (37, 48)
- Did the choice land, and is there a callback to plant? (66)
- New ruling? Tell the table, `gm-note.sh rules`. (31)

**Every few rounds**
- Energy: replies shorter, slower, off-topic? Change focus now. (54)
- Tension built and held? Time for a rest, or a new hook? (58, 61)
- Goals still clear to them? Say them in one line if not. (56)

**Never:** fudge · move a mystery's answer · tell one player a secret about another's PC ·
gate a core clue behind a roll · let a fight kill without warning · lie as the narrator.
